MINI GOLF MAKER COMMUNITY GUIDELINES

Last Updated: 06/04/26

Mini Golf Maker is built around creativity, sharing, and community-made content. These guidelines help keep the marketplace safe, welcoming, and enjoyable for everyone.

All content uploaded to Mini Golf Maker must comply with these Community Guidelines, our Terms of Service, and applicable laws.

1. Respect Intellectual Property

You may not upload content that you do not own or have permission to use.

This includes, but is not limited to:

  • Copyrighted models
  • Copyrighted textures
  • Copyrighted music or audio
  • Movies, television content, or artwork
  • Trademarks, logos, or brand assets
  • Other creators' content without permission

If you allow remixing or reuse of your content through Mini Golf Maker's permission systems, other users may use that content only within the permissions you grant.

2. No Adult or Explicit Content

Do not upload content that contains:

  • Pornography or sexual content
  • Nudity intended to be sexual in nature
  • Sexual acts or sexually explicit material
  • Graphic depictions of violence or gore

Content should be appropriate for the audience permitted by the platform and applicable age requirements.

3. No Hate, Harassment, or Bullying

Do not create or upload content that:

  • Targets individuals or groups with hate speech
  • Promotes discrimination or violence
  • Contains slurs or hateful symbols
  • Harasses, threatens, or intimidates others

4. No Scams or Deceptive Content

Do not attempt to mislead other players.

Examples include:

  • False advertising
  • Impersonating another creator
  • Misleading titles, descriptions, or thumbnails
  • Fraudulent marketplace activity

5. No Malicious or Harmful Content

You may not upload content intended to:

  • Disrupt gameplay
  • Exploit bugs or vulnerabilities
  • Circumvent platform systems
  • Interfere with other users' experiences

6. Respect Creator Permissions

Creators may control whether their content can be reused, modified, remixed, or reuploaded.

You may not:

  • Bypass creator permissions
  • Claim ownership of another creator's work
  • Remove required attribution information

7. Reporting and Enforcement

Users may report content that appears to violate these guidelines.

Reported content may be reviewed and may be hidden, restricted, removed, or otherwise moderated.

Repeated or severe violations may result in:

  • Marketplace restrictions
  • Content removal
  • Account enforcement actions
  • Suspension or termination of access to game services

8. Updates to These Guidelines

We may update these Community Guidelines from time to time.

Continued use of Mini Golf Maker after changes become effective constitutes acceptance of the updated guidelines.
