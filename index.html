What is
Mini Golf Maker

Mini Golf Maker is an immersive VR and AR experience where you can build, share, and compete on the mini golf courses of your dreams. Unleash your creativity by crafting unique courses, challenge friends by taking turns in the headset, and discover endless new mini golf adventures from the ever growing community. The AR mode is built to be one of the most comfortable and familiar experiences you can use to introduce newcomers to VR.
