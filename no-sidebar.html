1. Introduction

Welcome to Mini Golf Maker! This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, share, and protect your personal information when you use our game and related services ("Services"). By accessing or using our Services, you agree to the terms outlined in this Privacy Policy.

2. Information We Collect

Personal Information:

We do not collect personal information such as names, emails, or contact details. However, we may collect user-generated content, including in-game creations and gameplay statistics.

Device Information:

We gather device-related data such as type and operating system to enhance the gaming experience, particularly for AR and VR mode switching.

In-Game Data:

Gameplay data such as levels completed, items created, and future purchase-related data may be collected, with an option to opt out during initial game setup.

3. How We Use Your Information

Improvement:

Collected data helps us optimize and enhance gameplay experiences.

Analytics:

We use third-party analytics tools to analyze anonymized usage data for game improvements.

Marketing:

We do not send in-game marketing notifications. Players can opt-in to marketing updates via our Discord community.

4. Data Sharing and Security

Data Sharing:

We do not share personal data with third parties but may share anonymous analytics data for game improvements.

Security:

We use encrypted storage for in-game currency and user creations to ensure data security.

5. Your Rights and Choices

Access and Deletion:

You can request access, updates, or deletion of your in-game data by contacting us.

Opting Out:

Players can opt out of purchase data collection when launching the game and change this setting anytime.

6. Children’s Privacy

Mini Golf Maker is designed for all ages, including children under 13. We do not collect personal data from children without verifiable parental consent. Parents may contact us for data review or deletion.

7. Changes to Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.

8. Compliance

GDPR (EU Users):

EU users can access, update, or delete personal data and withdraw consent for processing.

CCPA (California Residents):

California residents may request data access, deletion, and opt out of data sharing.
