Privacy Policy for Mini Golf Maker

Last Updated: 04/17/25

1. Introduction

Welcome to Mini Golf Maker! This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, share, and protect your personal information when you use our game and related services (social media and website). By accessing or using our Services, you agree to the terms outlined in this Privacy Policy.

2. Information We Collect

Personal Information:

We collect Meta Account information such as your Meta username, Meta ID, and Meta friends. However, we may collect user-generated content, including in-game creations and gameplay statistics.

In-Game Data:

Gameplay data such as your purchase-related data may be collected, with an option to opt out during initial game setup.

3. How We Use Your Information

Security:

User data like your Meta ID allows us to safely conduct in app purchase transactions.

Improvement:

Collected data helps us optimize and enhance gameplay experiences. Your Meta account data like username and friends list are used to improve the multiplayer experience and searching on the in-game market place.

Analytics:

We use third-party analytics tools to analyze anonymized usage data for game improvements.

Marketing:

We display promotional content and offers within the game. These promotions are contained within the game and do not involve external notifications or messages. If players want to receive external marketing notifications they can opt-in to marketing updates via our Social Medias.

4. Data Sharing and Security

Data Sharing:

We do not share personal data with third parties but may share anonymous analytics data for game improvements.

Security:

We use industry-standard security measures, including encrypted storage for user creations and in-game currency to ensure data security.

5. Your Rights and Choices

Access and Deletion:

You can request access, updates, or deletion of any of your in-game data that we collect by contacting us (contact information below).

Opting Out:

Players can opt out of purchase data and in game statistic collection upon first launching the game and change this setting anytime.

6. Children’s Privacy

Mini Golf Maker is designed for all ages, including children under 13. We do not collect personal data from children without verifiable parental consent. Parents may contact us for data review or deletion.

7. Changes to Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.

8. Compliance

GDPR (EU Users):

EU users can access, update, or delete personal data and withdraw consent for processing.

CCPA (California Residents):

California residents may request data access, deletion, and opt out of data sharing.

9. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or your data, contact us by clicking the email address or through the messaging system below.
