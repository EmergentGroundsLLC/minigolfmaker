MINI GOLF MAKER PRIVACY POLICY

Last Updated: 06/04/26

1. Introduction

Welcome to Mini Golf Maker ("Mini Golf Maker," "we," "us," or "our"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect information when you use Mini Golf Maker and related services, including the in-game marketplace and user-generated content features.

By accessing or using Mini Golf Maker, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

2. Information We Collect

Account Information

Mini Golf Maker uses Meta account services for authentication. We may collect and store:

  • Meta User ID
  • Meta Username
  • Information necessary to identify account ownership

Gameplay and Marketplace Data

We may collect:

  • Courses or Blueprints that are uploaded to the marketplace
  • User-generated assets such as models, textures, images, audio, and other content uploaded to the game's marketplace
  • Marketplace listings and purchases
  • Course ratings and likes
  • In-game currency balances and transaction history
  • Downloaded course information
  • Gameplay statistics related to game functionality and service improvement

Moderation and Safety Data

We may collect:

  • Reports submitted against courses or creators
  • Records of moderation actions
  • Information related to guideline violations and enforcement actions

Technical Information

We may collect limited technical information necessary to operate and secure the game, including:

  • Device and platform information
  • Crash and error reports
  • Diagnostic information related to service performance

3. How We Use Information

We use collected information to:

  • Operate and maintain Mini Golf Maker
  • Authenticate users through Meta services
  • Process marketplace purchases and ownership records
  • Deliver purchased content
  • Detect fraud, abuse, exploits, and unauthorized activity
  • Moderate user-generated content
  • Improve game features and stability
  • Provide customer support
  • Comply with legal obligations

4. User-Generated Content

Mini Golf Maker allows users to upload and share content, including courses, models, textures, images, audio, and other creative works.

Content you publish may be visible to other players. You should not upload personal information or content you do not wish to share with other users.

Content metadata, including creator information, ownership information, remix permissions, and moderation information, may be stored as part of marketplace functionality.

5. Sharing of Information

We do not sell personal information.

We may share information:

  • With Meta and platform providers as required for game functionality
  • With service providers that assist in operating the game
  • When required by law, legal process, or governmental request
  • To protect the rights, safety, security, or property of users or Mini Golf Maker

6. Data Retention

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary to:

  • Operate the game
  • Maintain marketplace ownership records
  • Enforce our Terms of Service and Community Guidelines
  • Comply with legal obligations
  • Resolve disputes and investigate abuse

We may retain certain records after account deletion when necessary for legal, security, fraud-prevention, or moderation purposes.

7. Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards designed to protect information from unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or destruction.

No online service can guarantee absolute security.

8. International Users

Mini Golf Maker is available worldwide. Information may be processed and stored in countries other than your own.

By using the service, you acknowledge that your information may be transferred and processed internationally where permitted by law.

9. Future Features

Future versions of Mini Golf Maker may include features such as multiplayer services, voice communications, creator payout programs, automated moderation systems, artificial intelligence tools, recommendation systems, and additional social features.

If these features require material changes to our data practices, this Privacy Policy will be updated accordingly.

10. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights regarding your personal information, including:

  • Access to information we hold about you
  • Correction of inaccurate information
  • Deletion of certain information
  • Restriction or objection to certain processing activities
  • Data portability where required by law

Requests may be submitted using the contact information below.

11. Children's Privacy

Mini Golf Maker relies on Meta account services and is intended for users who meet Meta's account eligibility requirements and applicable legal requirements in their region.

If we become aware that information has been collected in violation of applicable law, we may take steps to delete such information.

12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time.

The updated version will be posted with a revised "Last Updated" date. Continued use of the service after changes become effective constitutes acceptance of the updated Privacy Policy.
